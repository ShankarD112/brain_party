WELCOME TO BRAIN PARTY

A little more grounded.

Each puzzle begins assembled, separates, swirls in a tornado, then falls onto the ground. You can skip this opening; it never counts toward your time.

  • Drag a piece across the ground plane. Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. F focuses your selection.
  • E lifts and Q lowers. You can also hold the Lift / Lower buttons. Arrow keys move across the ground. Hold Shift for finer movement.
  • Gravity starts on. Released pieces and floating clusters settle on the floor. Turn it off for a slow upward drift: smaller regions rise faster. Pieces stop at the floating ceiling. The floor and play-area boundary remain solid in either mode.
  • Height assist matches vertical placement when a true neighbor is close horizontally. The target glows gold; release or press Enter to connect. Turn assistance off for fully manual placement.
  • A sparkle and confirmation mean a correct connection. Joined pieces stay together and move as one. Every cluster remains movable. Start with any neighboring pair.
  • Shuffle loose pieces moves only unconnected pieces. Joined clusters, progress and time are preserved.

The completion bar counts connections, including floating clusters. The joined-region count shows how many regions belong to a connected cluster. Every piece must be connected to finish.

The timer starts on your first move or shuffle after the opening. Menu and pause stop both time and physics. The game uses floor-only physics: pieces do not physically collide with other pieces. Disconnected islands and both hemispheres can belong to the same anatomical label.
